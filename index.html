How much time should I set aside on shoot day?

Session length depends on the type of work. As a general guide from the company’s website, interior and exterior HDR photography with thirty or more images usually takes about forty-five minutes to one hour. Flash photography typically takes two to two and a half hours. A Matterport 3D tour adds about one additional hour. These are general ranges, so confirm the timing for your specific property when you book.

Build in buffer time. A four-bedroom home in Irvine with a flash session scheduled from midday until late afternoon leaves little room for a delayed trade appointment or a last-minute furniture move. A small cushion prevents rushed images and missed rooms.

What staging mistakes cause reshoots?

The most common problems are simple and visible in the final photos. Consider a Laguna Niguel kitchen where the counters held dishes, the blinds were closed on one side of the room, and a pet bowl sat near the doorway. The result was uneven light, cluttered surfaces and a distracting corner in several frames.

Before the shoot, open all blinds and curtains, turn on every light, clear counters and tables, hide cords and personal items, and check mirrors for reflections of the photographer or equipment. Remove trash cans from view and make beds neatly. Professional Real Estate Photography depends on good preparation as much as camera work, and a few minutes of setup often saves a reshoot.

How should I plan exterior and drone shots?

Exterior and aerial images need extra attention to conditions. Take a hillside home in Dana Point as an example. Trees that were fine from the street may block the ocean view from above, and neighboring properties may be visible in a wide aerial frame. Check the sky forecast, mark any obstructions, and discuss the desired angles with the photographer ahead of time.

House of Pix lists drone photography and aerial videography among its services, and the Google listing includes aerial photography as an additional category. If you want aerial work, ask whether drone flights are included in the session you are considering and confirm that they are allowed for your property. Real Estate Drone Photographer services should always be scheduled with a clear plan for timing, access, and what is being captured.

What is different about Airbnb photography?

Short-term rental photos serve guests, not only buyers, so the priorities shift. A two-bedroom condo in San Diego, for example, needs clear views of the sleeping areas, a kitchen that shows the available appliances, and a bathroom that looks clean and functional. Guests also want to know what the space is like at night, so ask whether the property should be shot with interior lights on at dusk.

Before an Airbnb shoot, make the beds, remove personal belongings, and set out only the amenities you actually provide. If the listing includes a patio, outdoor seating should be arranged and cleaned. Guests look for accuracy, so avoid staging items that will not be present during their stay.
